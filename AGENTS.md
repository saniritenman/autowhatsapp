# Project notes

AutoWhatsApp v2 is a manual-send app. Saving a reminder creates a Google Calendar event after the visitor authorizes Calendar access. Opening WhatsApp never means a message was sent. Only the user presses Send in WhatsApp and separately marks the reminder done.

Do not restore automatic senders, background WhatsApp sessions or PC workers. The previous source is preserved in archive/automatic-sender-2026-11-20. Do not modify BottleTrack. Keep administrator keys and OAuth tokens out of browser persistence, source and logs.
